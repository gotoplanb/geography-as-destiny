# Frontispiece

![*Travelers Among Mountains and Streams* (谿山行旅圖) — Fan Kuan, c. 1000 CE](fan-kuan-travelers-among-mountains.jpg)

*Travelers Among Mountains and Streams* (谿山行旅圖), Fan Kuan (范寬), Northern Song dynasty, c. 1000 CE. Ink on silk hanging scroll, 206.3 × 103.3 cm. National Palace Museum, Taipei.

The travelers and pack animals at the lower edge are barely visible against the mountain that occupies most of the composition. The painting is the framework's thesis in visual form: human activity is real and worth seeing, but the terrain is what the eye should rest on. Fan Kuan reportedly spent years living in the mountains he painted, studying how the terrain actually behaves before composing the picture. The receiver-side methodology applied to landscape a thousand years before Shannon named it.
