# Appendix C: Intellectual Lineage

The material that once sat here — the sketches of the intellectual traditions the framework synthesizes, from Shannon and Rogers through Wald, Ibn Khaldun, and the philosophy of science — has been moved to the front of the book. It now appears as the **Intellectual Lineage** section, between the Prologue and Chapter 1.

It was promoted to front matter on purpose: the lineage reads best *before* the argument rather than after it, because knowing these traditions gives the reader the mental substrate the rest of the book assumes. This slot is left in place only so the appendix lettering does not appear to skip; Appendices A, B, and D are unaffected.

See the **Intellectual Lineage** at the front for the full set of entries.
