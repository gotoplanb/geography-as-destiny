## The strong core — three moves

### 1. Continentality is a variance axis *independent of latitude*
The ocean's high heat capacity moderates coastal temperature swings (maritime climate, small annual range); continental interiors swing hard (large annual *and* interannual range). This is a geographic variable **orthogonal to latitude**: at the *same* latitude, maritime western Europe is mild and stable while the Eurasian interior/steppe is boom-and-bust. Cunliffe's Ch. 1 point (per Dave) is that a few miles from the coast you are already in a different variance regime. The framework has used climate-variance forcing throughout (185/188/227) but had **not named continentality as its own axis** — the standing geographic determinant of *how much the climate swings* in a given place. That is the piece this card adds: continentality sets the **amplitude of the climate forcing**, and amplitude (variance), not mean, is what makes a place a pump.

### 2. The scarcity-primed cultural ratchet — windfall converts to *expansion*, not settlement
High variance means a population is shaped by generations of scarcity: mobility-optimized, with **no sedentary cultural memory and no fixed-capital / storage / accumulation machinery**. When a swing to abundance arrives, such a population does **not** settle down and bank the surplus — it has no institutions for that — so it converts the windfall *directly into demographic and military expansion*. This is why a steppe abundance-swing produces an **outward pressure wave**, not just local population growth or in-place sedentarization. It is the **path-dependent / cultural** complement to card 010's **rational-economic** reading: 010 says mobility is simply the more productive optimization (so nomads rationally don't settle when rich); this card adds that even where settling *could* be rational, the scarcity-hardened culture *lacks the accumulation infrastructure to do anything with a windfall except expand*. Both mechanisms point the same way; together they explain the conversion of abundance into expansion. (Anti-eugenic, per 283/256/284: this is substrate- and culture-selection, not a claim about the people's nature — a stable-climate population handed the same windfall, with accumulation institutions, banks it instead.)

### 3. Resolving "same thing, pump out of Africa" — same fuel, different geometry, different output
Beta cautioned against collapsing the steppe pump into the Saharan pump. The resolution honors *both* Dave's intuition and Beta's caution, and it is pure **card-318 logic**:

- **Dave is right at the *driver* level.** Both are **high-variance zones**, and variance is the pump fuel in both. The Sahara margin swings green↔desert on the precession clock (card 298); the steppe interior swings on continentality. The shared essence Dave sensed is real: *pumps live where the environment is high-variance* (desert margins, continental interiors), **not** on stable temperate coasts.
- **Beta is right at the *output* level.** The **geometry that transduces the variance differs**, so the output differs:
  - **Saharan pump = concentration → sedentary** (card 047). A *bounded attractor* (the Nile, the only water punching north through the drying desert) funnels the dispersed population inward; density forces surplus management → agriculture → a sedentary civilization.
  - **Steppe pump = expansion → mobile** (this card). An *open grassland* with mobility preserved; the variance discharges as outward pressure waves (185/222). No bounded refugium to concentrate into, plus the mobility-optimized culture (move 2) → the swing exports population and military force rather than settling it.

So: **same fuel (environmental variance), different transducer (bounded-refugium geometry vs. open-mobile geometry), different output (sedentary concentration vs. mobile expansion).** This is card 318 at the pump scale — the climate variance is the scalar forcing; the local geographic *geometry* is the field that resolves it into either concentration or expansion. And it extends card 047's taxonomy cleanly: 047 already distinguishes the Saharan (contraction→Nile) pump from the Great Plains (steady-wet-abundance pulls nations inward → collision → horse decides) pump; the **steppe *variance* pump is a third, distinct variant** — driven by the *oscillation itself* rather than a steady abundance or a bounded contraction.

## Climate evidence — the steppe pump fires on variance in *both* directions
The paleoclimate record supports "variance is the pump" precisely because migration correlates with swings in *both* directions, not one:
- **Pluvial → enable.** Pederson et al. 2014 (Orkhon Valley tree-rings): a sustained wet spell **~1211–1225 CE** ("the Mongol pluvial") spiked grass production → war-horses/livestock → the material base of Mongol expansion. Abundance-swing → outward projection (Dave's "bathe in abundance" case).
- **Drought → push.** Recent work (e.g. a 2024 PNAS study) ties **first-millennium-CE** nomadic migrations to climate-driven *reductions* in steppe biomass — scarcity ejecting/raiding.
Both are the same pump: the **variance** (whichever way it swings) drives the outward wave — drought pushes people out, pluvial hands them the horses to project with. This *strengthens* Dave's core thesis (it's the variance, not a particular direction) while completing his abundance-only framing.

## Why this matters
- **Names the standing driver under the steppe pump.** The framework had climate-variance forcing (185/188/227) and the pump apparatus (047), but not **continentality** as the geographic axis that *sets* the variance — the reason the pumps sit in continental interiors and desert margins, not on coasts.
- **Formalizes the abundance→expansion ratchet** the archive demonstrated (010/256/284) but never named as discrete apparatus — and cleanly separates its cultural/path-dependent layer from 010's rational-economic layer.
- **Turns a would-be over-generalization into a sharper taxonomy.** "It's all the same pump" becomes "same variance fuel, different geometry, different output" — which is more defensible *and* more useful, and which is card 318's mechanism operating one level down.
