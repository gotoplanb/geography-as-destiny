## What's already yours (don't re-derive)
Dave's "power waning → xenophobia" is **established framework apparatus**, not new: **card 081 (The Imperial Tolerance Cycle)** already names it (expansion includes, contraction excludes; decline → scapegoating; *and* scapegoating accelerates decline by destroying the talent/trade base; Tang-Sogdian canonical; replicated Rome/Mongol/Ottoman→Armenian-genocide/British), **231 (Han Yu Moment)** makes xenophobic moral revival a *bellwether* (leads visible decline by ~1–2 generations), and **312** defines the decline (projection past peak) that opens the scapegoating window. This card supplies the three things 081 leaves implicit or lacks.

## Addition 1 — the target-selection mechanism: *why the trading minority* (Bonacich)
081 says decline scapegoats "visibly foreign, commercially successful, politically powerless" minorities; this names *why that profile is selected* and gives it its sociological name — **Edna Bonacich's "middleman minority."** A trading/middleman minority is the **structurally optimal scapegoat** because it maximizes all the scapegoat-selection variables at once:
- **Visible & distinct** (foreign dress, religion, language, endogamy) — easy to identify.
- **Economically conspicuous & wealthy** — visible prosperity reads as ill-gotten during a downturn.
- **A *middleman*** — positioned *between* the elite it serves and the masses it trades with, so it is **resented from both directions** (the classic middleman squeeze).
- **Politically powerless** — no army, no state of its own; safe to attack.
- **Cannot control the narrative** (the Wald link, 331/240) — it doesn't write the histories, so the conspiratorial accusation **sticks and becomes the record.**
So when 081's cycle turns to exclusion, the middleman minority is *who it lands on* — not by accident but because that profile scores highest on every dimension of "safe, satisfying, plausible target."

## Addition 2 — antisemitism as the most-lethal *instance* of a broad structural pattern (Dave's parallel)
Dave's catch is exact: the "traders/bankers secretly conspiring to bring down society" trope **is** the antisemitic structure — and it is *structural* (the middleman-minority position), not unique to any one people. The same shape recurs: **Sogdians** (Tang), **Jews** (Europe — the most lethal instance), **Overseas Chinese** (Southeast Asia), **Armenians** (Ottoman — already 081), **Indians** in East Africa, **Lebanese** in West Africa, **Parsis**, etc. The framework's move: name the **shared structure** (the boogeyman is *manufactured by the middleman position + the decline cycle*, not discovered in the group's behavior) **without trivializing the Holocaust** — antisemitism is the **most extreme and lethal** realization of the pattern, its severity a matter of degree *and* of specific historical amplifiers, held distinct even as the structure is shared.

## Addition 3 — the concrete Tang anchors (new; 081 has the pattern, not these)
- **An Lushan was himself of Sogdian-Turkic descent** (a Chinese general of Iranian-and-Turkic origin). **The rebel whose 755–763 rebellion triggered the anti-Sogdian backlash was *himself* Sogdian-descended** — a genuinely poignant detail 081 omits: the scapegoating had a *real* originating figure, then **generalized** from "the Sogdian-descended rebel" to "Sogdians / foreigners in general" (the leap from a real instance to a collective conspiracy is the boogeyman move itself).
- **Post-755 anti-Sogdian backlash → self-erasure:** Sogdian communities were treated with suspicion; **Sogdians began concealing their foreign origins** — the War Minister **An Chongzhang changed his surname in 756, "ashamed to bear the same name" as An Lushan.** The scapegoated minority *erasing itself* — the human face of card **076's Sogdian identity dissolution**, and the Wald "can't control the record" made literal (they didn't defend their name; they dropped it).
- **Yangzhou massacre (760):** foreign Arab and Persian merchants slaughtered; the sources state their **"wealth incurred xenophobic feelings… they were scapegoated as the Tang dynasty declined… targeted for being foreign and wealthy."** The middleman-minority profile, verbatim.
- **Guangzhou massacre (879):** in the terminal Tang decline, Huang Chao's rebels massacre the foreign-merchant community (Arabs, Persians, Jews, Zoroastrians, Christians). The connective international-port community destroyed — hastening the end of Guangzhou's role as a great trade hub.

## The self-destructive irony + the double erasure (sharpening 081)
Two things worth stating sharply: (1) **scapegoating the connective merchant minority kills the golden goose** — the Sogdian/foreign-merchant networks *were* the trade that enriched the Tang; massacring them destroys the very infrastructure recovery would need, accelerating decline (081's spiral, made concrete by the massacres). (2) **Double erasure:** the middleman minority is both *scapegoated* (blamed) and *silenced* (can't control the record) — so it disappears twice, once in life (concealment, massacre) and once in memory (the accusation becomes the surviving narrative). That is why the Sogdians, one of history's great commercial networks, are remembered dimly and mostly through others' records (the 331 Parthia/Wald pattern again).

## Calibration / open items
1. **The 740s Sogdian *discontent* was likely real grievance (agency), NOT scapegoating — don't build the boogeyman case on it.** In the turbulent 740s (Abbasid Revolution 747–750; Umayyad/Turkic contest over Transoxiana), Sogdian merchants had **genuine material grievances** against whichever authority disrupted their trade — that is Sogdian *agency*, not a scapegoating narrative *about* Sogdians. Dave's initial "smells like antisemitism" pattern-match **over-reads the 740s case**; the boogeyman/scapegoat pattern is properly anchored in the **post-755** episodes (backlash, Yangzhou, Guangzhou). Distinguish *real grievance (the minority acting)* from *scapegoating (the majority blaming)* — Beta's exact point; they are different claims needing different evidence.
2. **The "cosmopolitan Tang → xenophobic Tang flip" is a contested oversimplification — hold at the episode level.** Recent scholarship challenges *both* the "golden age of openness" and the "precipitous descent into xenophobia" myths; there was no clean civilizational flip. So anchor the pattern in **documented episodes** (the massacres, the anti-Sogdian backlash, the Han Yu memorial), *not* in a wholesale "open→closed" transformation of Tang society.
3. **Catch the structure; don't trivialize the Holocaust.** Antisemitism is the most-lethal instance of the pattern, not merely one interchangeable case; hold its distinct severity.
4. **Massacre figures are soft.** The Guangzhou "120,000" is from Arab sources (Abu Zayd) and is likely inflated; the massacres are real, the numbers approximate.
5. **This extends 081; it is not a second cycle card.** The *when* (decline turns exclusionary) is 081; the *bellwether* is 231; this is the *who/why-this-target* + the concrete anchors + the theoretical name.
