## Beta's Confirmation with Substantive Complexity

**Direct confirmation of the macro-pattern prediction:**

> Sharp prediction and the science largely confirms it — with some fascinating nuances worth knowing before Reich gets there.
>
> Non-African modern humans carry roughly 1-4% Neanderthal DNA. The percentage varies by population — East Asians carry slightly more than Europeans, suggesting multiple mixing events at different times and places. Sub-Saharan Africans carry essentially none, which confirms the mixing happened after modern humans left Africa.

**Substantive complexity beyond the macro-pattern prediction:**

> Multiple mixing events not one. The Altai Neanderthal — the Denisova Cave population — mixed with modern humans separately from the Western European Neanderthals. The Denisovans — a separate archaic population discovered through aDNA from that same Altai cave — mixed with the ancestors of Melanesians and Aboriginal Australians, who carry up to 5% Denisovan DNA.

**The Altai as deep-time robust frontier:**

> The Altai as the mixing zone for multiple archaic and modern human populations is the framework's robust frontier argument pushed back 50,000 years. The same terrain feature that produced the Afanasievo leapfrog, the Tocharian isolation, and the Silk Road corridor dynamics was already producing population contact and mixing events in the Paleolithic.
>
> The Altai has been a friction zone and contact point for as long as humans have been moving across Eurasia. The specific populations change. The terrain keeps doing the same thing.

**The framework's thermodynamic-displacement-with-genetic-legacy reading:**

> The pressure differential between expanding modern human populations with more sophisticated toolkit and the already-stressed Neanderthal populations in marginal refugia was too large to sustain Neanderthal population viability over time. The mixing happened at the contact zones. The Neanderthal population couldn't maintain itself against the demographic and competitive pressure even with occasional interbreeding.
>
> Not genocide. Not peaceful blending. Thermodynamic displacement with genetic legacy.
>
> The Neanderthals are the engine hits. The 1-4% of their DNA we carry is the signal that survived in the returning bombers.
