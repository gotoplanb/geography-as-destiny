# Lapita Pottery as Cultural DNA — Material Culture as Encoded Signal Confirming Population Connection (Third Instrument Alongside Genome and Framework)

**Source:** Beta seminar discussion, 2026-06-16, during Dave's listening of Christina Thompson, *Sea People: The Puzzle of Polynesia* (William Collins, 2019), Part 3
**Type:** connection
**Relevant to:** Chapter 1 (Reich cluster, two-instruments methodology), Foreword (Shannon receiver-side reading), Pacific cascade cluster (cards 278, 289)

## Content

The Lapita cultural complex (c. 1500–500 BCE, named for the type site on Foué Peninsula, New Caledonia) is identified archaeologically by a distinctive ceramic tradition: dentate-stamped pottery with intricate geometric and sometimes anthropomorphic decoration, executed in shared techniques and motif vocabularies across a vast Pacific spread. The geographic distribution covers the Bismarck Archipelago, Solomon Islands, Vanuatu, New Caledonia, Fiji, Tonga, and Samoa. The window of appearance is narrow — most of the geographic spread shows Lapita pottery appearing within roughly 1100–900 BCE, after which the tradition continues for several centuries before declining.

Thousands of miles of Pacific. A few centuries of appearance. Shared decorative motifs, shared manufacturing techniques, shared forms. This is the cultural equivalent of a genetic signature. The pottery confirms population connection through encoded material culture the way Yamnaya ancestry confirms population connection through the genome.

The framework's two-instruments methodology (cards 273, 275) treats genomic analysis and framework analysis as complementary instruments measuring the same population history. The Lapita pottery case extends this. *Material culture is a third instrument.* The decorative motifs, manufacturing chains, and form vocabularies are Shannon-encoded signals carrying information about transmission patterns, social organization, and population connection. The receiver-side discipline reads them as such — not as decorative randomness, not as art-for-art's-sake, but as encoded population signal that can be triangulated against the genomic and framework readings.

For the Pacific cascade specifically this matters because the genomic signal of the Out-of-Taiwan expansion is well-documented (Austronesian / East Asian ancestry across the Pacific with Melanesian admixture acquired in passage) but the *timing* and *cultural-transmission pattern* of the Lapita expansion is harder to read from the genome alone. The pottery provides the timing and pattern detail the genome can't. The two instruments together confirm what neither could confirm alone — that the Lapita expansion was a unified cultural-and-population movement carrying a shared decorative vocabulary across the Pacific in a narrow window after the outrigger friction collapse.

The framework's broader claim — that population history is best read through multiple instruments rather than any single instrument — is strengthened by the Lapita case. The Yamnaya expansion has genome + archaeology + linguistic reconstruction as its three instruments. The Lapita expansion has genome + archaeology (pottery specifically) + linguistic reconstruction (Proto-Oceanic; card 292) as the parallel three. The same triangulation methodology operates across both cases, in independent substrates, producing convergent reconstructions.

## Why this matters

This refines the framework's two-instruments methodology in three ways.

1. **Material culture as a third instrument.** Cards 273 and 275 frame the framework's apparatus as a two-instrument triangulation (genome + framework). The Lapita case demonstrates that material culture — when read with Shannon receiver-side discipline as encoded signal rather than as decoration — is a third complementary instrument. Worth carrying as a methodological refinement.

2. **The framework's apparatus is genuinely general across substrates.** The Yamnaya case is reconstructed from genome + archaeology + linguistic reconstruction. The Lapita case is reconstructed from the same three instrument types, operating in an independent substrate (Pacific maritime), confirming a parallel expansion logic. The framework is not Eurasia-specific; the methodology works wherever the three instruments are available.

3. **The Pacific cascade now has multiple convergent confirmations.** Cards 278, 289, and 290 together reconstruct the Pacific cascade through outrigger as friction-collapse technology, Taiwan-resident-redirect as launch mechanism, and Lapita pottery as cultural-DNA signature of the actual expansion. The three readings converge on the same reconstruction, which is the strongest available form of framework validation.
