## Calibration

1. **Dave's prediction was at macro-pattern level, not specific-identity level.** Dave predicted *the existence and rough character* of a geographic feature operating as a barrier for forager populations — open prairie / scrubland adjacent to forest, requiring domesticated-animal mobility to traverse. He did not predict "the Kalahari Desert specifically." The macro-pattern prediction was confirmed; the specific-identity correspondence is a bonus the framework should not over-claim. Saussure-mode prediction-validation operates at macro-pattern level by design.

2. **The Kalahari is semi-arid scrubland and savanna, not desert in the Sahara sense.** Beta's characterization ("semi-arid scrubland") is more precise than "desert" as commonly understood. The Kalahari is technically a semi-desert / savanna with substantial vegetation; its barrier function operates through water-scarcity and seasonal-hydrology mechanics, not Sahara-style absolute aridity. The framework's reading operates correctly at the macro-pattern level (barrier requiring water-carrying toolkit); the specific characterization should hold this precision.

3. **The "three-wave African agricultural migration" framing is substantive but should hold scientific complexity.** Mainstream African aDNA and archaeological scholarship documents substantial complexity in the patterns of agricultural and pastoral spread into sub-Saharan Africa across the Holocene. Three-wave is a useful macro-pattern summary; specific within-wave timing, geographic routes, and population compositions involve substantial within-discipline complexity. The framework's reading operates at macro-pattern level.

4. **The ~30,000-year date for the KhoeSan split should hold appropriate dating uncertainty.** Specific dating of population-genetic divergence events involves substantial methodological complexity (molecular clock calibration; ancestral-population modeling; recent revisions of human mutation rates). The 30,000-year window is mainstream paleogenetic consensus; specific dating refinement continues in the literature. The framework's reading operates at macro-pattern level (deep-time divergence before the velocity threshold's pastoralist friction collapse).

5. **The Bantu-expansion-as-bridging-event framing is substantive but should hold complexity.** The Bantu expansion is itself a multi-millennial multi-route process that mainstream scholarship continues to investigate. The framework's reading articulates the eventual bridging of the Kalahari frontier by Bantu-derived populations; specific Bantu-expansion reconstruction (timing, routes, populations) is substantively complex beyond what this card engages.

6. **The forager-vs-pastoralist mobility-technology threshold framing should not over-claim.** Pastoralism is not the *only* technology that can bridge water-scarcity barriers; specific cases involve various pre-pastoralist water-management technologies (carried-water vessels, seasonal-migration patterns matched to water availability, etc.). The framework's reading articulates pastoralism as the canonical case of a friction-collapse technology operating below the velocity threshold; specific case-by-case reconstruction may identify other relevant technologies. The macro-pattern reading is robust; specific within-case complexity should be acknowledged.

7. **"The framework predicted the geographic feature before you looked at the map" is rhetorically powerful but should hold its scope precisely.** What was predicted: the existence and rough character of a barrier feature operating on forager populations. What was confirmed: the Kalahari Desert as the specific instance. The framework's apparatus is substantively predictive at macro-pattern level on substrate-driven mechanisms; it is not omniscient about specific terrain features. This card demonstrates the framework's predictive capacity working correctly on a specific case; future framework engagement should maintain the appropriate epistemic scope.
